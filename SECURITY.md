# Security Policy

BLCReverseLab is intended for software you own, are explicitly authorized to assess, or are permitted to analyze in a lab/CTF/research context.

Please report vulnerabilities in BLCReverseLab through GitHub's private security reporting feature when available. Do not include third-party secrets, proprietary binaries, credentials, or exploit material against systems you do not control.

Out of scope for this project: anti-cheat bypasses, stealth/evasion intended to defeat third-party controls, credential theft, signature/integrity bypass automation, and online-cheating automation.
